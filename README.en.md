# Learning_at_ruc

Study materials for the **equivalent-academic-qualification master's program (同等学力申硕) at Renmin University of China (RUC)**, covering the Computer Science / Big Data / Management Science tracks. For personal study use only.

## Contents

- `01_网络与通信/` ~ `21_云计算与大数据/` — per-course review materials (numbered to align with `Run_RUC_Course`).
- `15_国考计算机综合/` — national exam (计算机综合): syllabus, math (combinatorics + discrete math), and two-out-of-five electives.
- `课程资料/` — download links for course slides and lecture recordings.
- `论文写作资料/` — thesis writing: proposal, defense, templates, literature tools.
- `RUC.md` — FAQ (enrollment, qualification certificate, exams, teacher contacts).
