# Learning_at_ruc

Study materials for the **equivalent-academic-qualification master's program (同等学力申硕) at Renmin University of China (RUC)**, covering the Computer Science / Big Data / Management Science tracks. For personal study use only.

This repository merges two sources:

- the original `Learning_at_ruc` repo by senior `jasnei`;
- the `RUC_CourseMaterials` repo by senior `Flamindle`.

## Contents

- `01_网络与通信/` … `14_学术规范与论文写作/` — the 14 Computer Science courses (numbered by recommended study order).
- `15_国考计算机综合/` — the national exam (计算机综合): math + two-out-of-five electives.
- `17_大数据推荐与决策/` … `21_云计算与大数据/` — Big Data courses.
- `22_高级管理学/` … `27_CIO与IT治理/` — Management Science courses.
- `管科其他课程/` — extra economics/management courses (game theory, macroeconomics).
- `课程资料/` — download links for course slides and lecture recordings.
- `论文写作资料/` — thesis writing (proposal, defense, templates, literature tools).
- `学校安排/`、`招生简章/` — course schedules and enrollment brochures.

## Exams

- **题库考** (college, closed-book, from a question bank) — 4 courses.
- **期末考试** (instructor, open/closed-book) — the rest.
- **国考** (national, closed-book) — English + 计算机综合, results valid 4 years.
